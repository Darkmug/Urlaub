# Urlaub

German Travel Itineraries for Solo Travelers

## Overview

This repository contains comprehensive travel plans for young solo travelers in Germany during the Christmas season (December 16-25, 2025).

## Contents

- **[خطط_الرحلات_ألمانيا.md](./خطط_الرحلات_ألمانيا.md)** - Detailed travel itineraries in Arabic

## Travel Plans Included

The document provides 5 different short-trip plans (3-4 days each) with detailed daily schedules:

1. **Black Forest (Freiburg)** - 4 days, 379 EUR
   - Skiing, Christmas markets, waterfalls, mountain adventures
   
2. **Bavarian Alps (Berchtesgaden)** - 3 days, 287 EUR
   - Eagle's Nest, Königssee lake, salt mine, alpine views
   
3. **Romantic Road (Rothenburg)** - 3 days, 245 EUR
   - Medieval town, historic walls, Christmas markets, countryside cycling
   
4. **Dresden & Saxon Switzerland** - 4 days, 330 EUR
   - Art and culture, Christmas markets, hiking in Saxon Switzerland National Park
   
5. **Lübeck & Baltic Sea** - 3 days, 260 EUR
   - Hanseatic history, marzipan, coastal adventures, maritime Christmas markets

## Key Features

Each plan includes:
- Budget-friendly hostel accommodation suggestions
- Detailed daily itineraries with activities
- Adventure and unique experiences
- Christmas markets (Weihnachtsmärkte)
- Budget breakdown (all under 500 EUR)
- Transportation recommendations
- Cultural and natural activities

## Target Audience

- Young solo travelers
- Adventure seekers
- Budget-conscious travelers (max 500 EUR per trip)
- Those seeking quiet, beautiful cities and nature
- Travelers interested in authentic German experiences

## Language

All travel plans are written in Arabic to serve Arabic-speaking travelers planning their German adventures.
